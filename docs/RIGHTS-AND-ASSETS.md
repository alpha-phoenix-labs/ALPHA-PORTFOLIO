# Rights & Asset Register

Use this checklist before adding an image, logo, screenshot, text excerpt, or other third-party material to the public portfolio.

| Asset | Source / creator | Rights basis / permission | Attribution required | Approved for public repo? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Portfolio-generated concept mockup | Record generation/source | Confirm applicable tool terms | Label as concept mockup | Pending review |
| Live website screenshot | Record exact URL and capture date | Confirm authorization / applicable rights | Identify as screenshot; avoid implying endorsement | Pending review |
| Logo or brand mark | Record rights holder | Obtain permission or confirm legal basis | As required | No, until reviewed |
| Photography / illustrations | Record source and license | Verify license and scope | As required by license | No, until reviewed |
| Original website copy | Record source and author | Confirm permission / legal basis | As required | No, until reviewed |

## Publication rules

- Publicly accessible does not automatically mean free to reuse.
- Do not represent generated mockups as screenshots of a live site.
- Do not use third-party branding in a way that implies endorsement or an official relationship.
- Do not include credentials, personal data, private operational details, or non-public configuration.
- Keep evidence of permissions and license terms with the project records.
- If rights are uncertain, omit the asset and use an original neutral alternative.

This checklist is an operational control, not legal advice or a determination that any particular asset is cleared.
